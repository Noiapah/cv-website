Case study · ReuseID Demo

A secure identity flow, made tangible.

The brief

Deliver a full-stack demonstration for Signicat inside an existing technical ecosystem, translating a complex authentication product into a credible online-banking journey.

What I worked with

Vue 3, TypeScript, microfrontends, Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, relational databases, JUnit, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, GCP, and GitLab CI/CD.

Key learning

Authentication alone is not enough: every protected resource also needs ownership and permission validation. Clear architecture made those rules easier to test and maintain.

Delivery

The team worked through Scrum, sprint planning, code reviews, documentation, stakeholder communication, and careful scope decisions to protect core quality.
